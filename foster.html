Foster

Foster a dog. Save a life.

Fostering is the single most valuable thing you can do in rescue. We can only save these majestic dogs if we have a temporary home for them while we find their permanent one.

Apply to fosterFoster FAQ

Why it matters

When you open your home to a desperate dog, you give them love and security. In return, you get to watch them grow, gain confidence and bloom into a great companion. You started out rescuing the dog, but often it's the dog that rescues you.

It isn't always easy, and sometimes it's downright inconvenient. But when you see them walk off into the sunset with their new family, it's always worth it.

How fostering works

  1. Apply

    Fill out the foster application so we can learn about your family and home.

  2. Get matched

    We match you with a foster dog that suits your family and home.

  3. Sign the contract

    Once approved, complete the foster contract. The dog remains GPRS's while in your care.

  4. Love them forward

    The dog lives with you as family until we find their permanent home.

Foster FAQ

What do I feed a foster dog?
If you have other dogs, you can feed your foster the same food. Great Pyrenees are giant breed dogs with surprisingly slow metabolisms, so they usually need less food than people imagine. It's better to feed an adult twice a day rather than all at once. Food can be provided if you need it.
Are shots and medical care included?
Yes. Your foster dog gets all vaccinations, including rabies, and is spayed or neutered at GPRS vets, paid for by GPRS. Any medications the dog is on when it comes to you are provided.
What if my foster dog develops a medical problem?
Call our dog coordinator. They'll decide with you whether the dog needs to see one of our approved vets, and direct you to the closest one. Once the dog has been seen and treatment is approved by GPRS, we pay for it.
What if I want to adopt my foster dog?
Contact us as soon as possible so you can apply before anyone else is approved. We'll remove the dog from the website, you complete the adoption contract and pay the adoption fee, and your new baby becomes yours.
Where do you need fosters?
We have foster homes in Texas and the Pacific Northwest (Oregon and Washington). The need in Texas is greatest. Many times we can't pull a dog from a shelter without a foster home lined up.
