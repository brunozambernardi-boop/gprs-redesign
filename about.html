About us

Saving Great Pyrenees whose lives are in danger

Based in Texas, Great Pyrenees Rescue Society is a volunteer-run, foster-based 501(c)(3) animal rescue dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation and adoption of Great Pyrenees dogs.

Our story

GPRS was founded by Malise Saucier in 2006 as a coalition of rescuers placing Great Pyrenees and Pyr mixes across the country, with rescues in several states where shelter kill rates are high for the breed.

Most of our dogs come from kill shelters in Central Texas, South Texas and the Greater Houston area. They go to foster homes in Texas and the Pacific Northwest, where they're evaluated and rehabilitated until we find them a permanent home. We have a long history of successful adoptions in Texas and the Northwest, supported by a monthly transport from Texas to the Pacific NW.

We adopt to families in Texas, Washington, Oregon, Idaho, California, New Mexico and Arizona.

There are far more wonderful, majestic, deserving Great Pyrenees in Texas than we have room for. The only way we can save them is with your help: foster homes, donations, volunteers, and families ready to adopt.

How we work

  • Foster-based: no kennels. Every dog lives in a home, so we know them well.
  • All-volunteer: 100% of donations go directly to the dogs.
  • Fully vetted: vaccinations, rabies, and spay/neuter before adoption.
