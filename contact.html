Need to surrender your Pyr?

We have foster homes in Texas, Oregon and Washington that can take owner surrenders. Fill out the owner surrender form with everything about your Great Pyrenees, and a volunteer will contact you if a foster home is open. If you can keep your Pyr for a little while, that helps while we look for an open foster.

Outside those states? See the list of Pyr rescues at National Pyrs.

Owner surrender form

Know of a Pyr in a shelter?

We have limited volunteers and can't call about every shelter dog in Texas. Before sending us a dog, please:

  1. Call the shelter or person who posted it and ask whether the dog is already safe (adopted, reclaimed or on a rescue hold).
  2. Find out if the dog is good with other dogs, cats and kids, if that's known.
  3. Post the dog on your Facebook page and ask for a foster home. Often we can't pull a dog without one.
  4. Ask people to fill out our foster application.

If a dog isn't in Texas, Washington, Oregon or Idaho, we can't help, but National Pyrs lists rescues in other states.

The simple, painful truth is that we don't have enough foster homes for every dog in a shelter. Please consider fostering.
