Get involved

Lend a hand to a Gentle Giant

Even if now isn't the right time to bring home a Pyr of your own, there are so many ways to help, and many of them can be done remotely. Bring your talents, gain experience, and help a dog's dream of a permanent home come true.

Fill out the volunteer form

Hands-on in Spring, Texas

Want to work directly with our beautiful Great Pyrenees? We have positions right here in Spring, TX:

  • Pet transport
  • Dog walking
  • Kennel duty
  • Grooming and bathing

Remote or near you

Whether you live far away or nearby, we have volunteer work in Texas and remote options, from liaison roles to creative and marketing work.

Fill out our form and we'll get back to you about what's available.

Open roles

Time estimates are per week.

5–10 hrs/wk

Shelter Liaison

Call shelters about dogs in danger and keep the group updated on their status.

5–10 hrs/wk

Foster Liaison

Keep tabs on fosters and their needs. Help track vetting, collect dog photos, and find out what adopters need to know.

1–2 hrs/wk

Vet Liaison

Contact vet offices to explore relationships and discounted pricing.

10+ hrs/wk

Adopter Liaison

Check in with adoptive families, provide support and resources, and share updates and pictures.

3–5 hrs/wk

Transport Coordinator

Coordinate transport of dogs to airports and other locations for out-of-state adoptions.

3–5 hrs/wk

Volunteer Coordinator

Arrange volunteers for events and to help foster homes with many dogs.

3–5 hrs/wk

Public Relations

Community outreach and education through events, advertising and media.

1–3 hrs/wk

Website Coordinator

Maintain the website front page by posting new stories and other tasks.

5–10 hrs/wk

Internet Research

Research medical conditions, medicine costs, dog supplies and other topics.

3–5 hrs/wk

Listing Coordinator

Polish write-ups from fosters and coordinate listings with our pet portal updater.

5–10 hrs/wk

Fundraiser

Come up with creative ideas to raise money, then put them into action.

1–3 hrs/wk

Grant Writer

Complete grant applications to agencies that fund rescue work.

3–5 hrs/wk

Pet Supplier Liaison

Contact pet companies for donations or discounts on flea and worm treatments, crates, food and more.
